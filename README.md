# Privacy-policy-for-DriftsDot
Privacy Policy
Effective Date: November 25, 2026
App Name: DriftDots
At DriftDots, we believe that your personal thoughts and daily reflections are sacred and belong exclusively to you. Our privacy philosophy is absolute: your data stays on your device.
1. Data Storage & Local Security
•
All journal entries, prompts, dates, and mood selections are saved exclusively and securely in a local database on your device.
•
We do not operate cloud servers, external databases, or remote backup systems. Your personal reflections never leave your phone or tablet.
2. Device Permissions
•
DriftDots requires zero sensitive permissions. We do not access your camera, microphone, photo gallery, location, contacts, or device accounts.
3. Advertising & Tracking
•
No Ads: DriftDots is 100% ad-free.
•
No Analytics or SDKs: We do not use third-party analytics, crash reporting trackers, or user behavior tracking software. You are never tracked.
4. Children’s Privacy
•
DriftDots does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. Because all information remains entirely local to your device, no data is ever transmitted to us.
5. Policy Updates
•
Any future updates to this policy will be reflected on this page. Because we do not collect or store user data on external servers, your privacy is unconditionally guaranteed under all circumstances.
6. Contact
•
For any questions regarding privacy or app features, please reach out via our support channel.
